Style: stylised dark-fantasy CARTOON pixel art with a comic-book feel, not realism. Exaggerated, caricatured proportions: the head is huge (almost half the body), hands, boots, beards and weapons are oversized and chunky. Bold simple shapes and readable silhouettes, thick dark ink outlines, flat colour areas with hard cel-shaded shadows (two or three flat tones per material), very little texture noise. Expressive, rubbery faces with big brows and noses. Grim and grimy mood: soot, dents, scars, muted iron and earth tones with punchy gold, crimson and ember accents. Think a dark comic or animated series, playful exaggeration but still menacing. Pixel-art clusters, no dithering, no airbrushed gradients, no photorealistic detail. Must read clearly when shrunk to 48 pixels.
